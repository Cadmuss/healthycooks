# Sesame salmon rice bowl — quick route

Updated 30 September 2026. Japanese-inspired healthycooks adaptation; no creator endorsement.

## Timing and ingredients

About 25 minutes is an estimate for two portions, thawed salmon, ready-cooked brown rice and one air-fryer batch. It includes washing/cutting, dressing and assembly; vegetables and rice are prepared while fish cooks. More portions, slow prep or appliance preheating can take longer. Oven route: about 30 minutes. Fresh brown rice: approximately 55–65 minutes or the complete cooker cycle; never advertise dry brown rice as a 25-minute meal.

Two portions: 420 g cooked brown rice, 260 g raw boneless salmon (two approximately 130 g fillets), 300 g broccoli, 200 g cucumber, 150 g carrots, 5 ml rapeseed oil, 15 ml reduced-salt soy, 15 ml rice vinegar, 10 g toasted sesame and 10 g ginger. Fresh-rice equivalent is approximately 140 g dry; measure the required cooked amount rather than treating the yield as exact. Nutrition is a rough manual illustration (670 kcal, 40 g protein, 74 g carbs, 25 g fat, 9 g fibre per portion), not a validated calculation or the source publisher's nutrition.

## Consistent flow

1. Choose ready-cooked pouch, safely chilled rice, fresh stovetop or rice cooker. Pouches stay unopened until serving; chilled home-cooked rice remains refrigerated until reheating.
2. Wash and cut vegetables once: broccoli ~3 cm, carrots ~3 mm, cucumber half-moons. Cucumber stays separate.
3. Peel/grate ginger and mix with all soy and vinegar. This dressing never touches raw fish.
4. Pat thawed boneless fillets dry, check remaining bones and brush with all oil. Clean raw-fish tools and hands.
5. Air fryer: 200°C, about 8–10 min, first check at 8. Follow appliance preheat instructions. Oven: 180°C conventional / 160°C fan, 10–15 min, first check at 10. Thickness and load affect time. Work on the next steps while cooking.
6. Steam or boil the already-cut broccoli/carrot. Boiling gives carrots 2 min before broccoli; then 4–5 min after the water returns to a gentle boil. Drain. Do not cook cucumber.
7. Heat only the rice being eaten now; follow pouch directions or heat chilled rice with a splash of water, stirring until steaming throughout. Fresh hot rice needs no reheating.
8. Assemble all components, then add the clean dressing and all sesame. Follow separate storage route for future meals.

Fish checks: thickest part steaming hot, opaque and easily flaked; continue cooking if translucent/cool. No mandatory thermometer added. Time alone is not doneness.

## Meal prep

Store salmon/vegetables separately from cold toppings. For the quick meal-prep route, keep rice pouches unopened until serving and follow their labels. Alternatively cook fresh rice and cool promptly. Do not cool/store reheated rice for another meal. Cool fish/vegetables and refrigerate within 2 hours at 4°C or below; plan to eat next day. Fresh rice has its original 24-hour clock. Freeze later portions promptly, thaw in fridge, reheat only once until steaming throughout. Use chilled transport with an ice pack.
