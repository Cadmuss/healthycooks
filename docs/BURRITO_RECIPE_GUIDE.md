# Chicken & cheese burritos — Mexico pilot

Updated 30 September 2026. Replaces the India chana-chaat pilot, at the user's request. Display name remains healthycooks.

## Recipe design

Mexican-inspired, not claimed as a traditional or creator-approved recipe. No beans. A portion is **two small wholemeal burritos**, using the ~45 g wraps available in the selected FairPrice pack. The two-portion ingredient list is canonical: four wraps, 200 g raw skinless chicken, 150 g cooked brown rice, 200 g bell pepper before trimming, 80 g red onion before peeling, 40 g cheddar, 10 ml canola oil, 2 g paprika, 1 g cumin and 40 g mild salsa.

[Anna Glover / Good Food, Easy chicken burritos](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/easy-chicken-burritos) is the assembly inspiration. The source uses cooked chicken, beans, avocado and optional sour cream. Our version omits those latter three, starts with raw chicken, uses smaller wholemeal wraps and includes pepper. Instructions are newly written, not copied wholesale. The source's published nutrition does not describe our adaptation.

The quick route is about 30 minutes with safely precooked rice and one filling batch. It is not advertised as 15 minutes from dry brown rice. Fresh rice can make the full meal 55–65 minutes or longer depending on appliance and batch size. Nutrition (605 kcal, 38 g protein, 68 g carbs, 20 g fat, 8 g fibre per two-burrito portion) is a rough manual illustration, NOT an ingredient-level verified calculation; actual wrap brands and yields vary. Do not imply health certification or guaranteed savings.

## Consistent eight-step flow

1. Choose one rice route. Canonical weight is cooked: 75 g per portion, about 25 g dry equivalent. Already-cooked rice remains chilled until step 5. Fresh rice starts first; time filling for the end of the rice cycle. Respect a cooker's minimum batch, portion out the required amount and store extra separately.
2. Prepare pepper and onion before raw chicken. Similar 5 mm strips; shorten long strips for small wraps. Grate cheese and keep it separate.
3. Slice chicken into approximately 1 cm-thick, 3–4 cm-long strips. Coat chicken and prepared vegetables ONCE with all the oil and spices. Treat the whole mixture as raw poultry. Clean tools/hands before touching ready-to-eat food.
4. Choose pan or air fryer for that same mixture. Pan: about 8–12 min, first-check timer 8 min. Air fryer: 200°C, about 15 min, turn around 5 and 10 min. Work in batches if crowded; do not multiply cooking time by portions. The timer prompts a doneness check, not an automatic declaration of safety.
5. For immediate eating, reheat chilled rice once until steaming throughout, and soften wraps separately. Freshly hot rice just needs fluffing. Microwave or stovetop directions are offered; foil never goes in a microwave. For meal prep, skip warming/assembly/toasting and store fresh components instead.
6. Divide the rice, cooked filling, cheese and salsa evenly into two wraps per portion. Leave edge room; serve excess filling alongside if the wraps are smaller than expected. Do not add beans or creator-video toppings.
7. Fold both sides, tuck the near edge around the filling and roll seam-side down.
8. Optional brief dry-pan toast, 1–2 min per side, or keep soft. This finishes already-hot filling; it is not a reheating method for a cold/frozen burrito.

Pan technique: [Good Food, Easy chicken fajitas](https://www.bbcgoodfoodme.com/recipes/easy-chicken-fajitas/). Air fryer: [Bored of Lunch](https://boredoflunch.com/recipes/air-fryer-chicken-fajitas/), whose actual method specifies 200°C for 15 minutes (its FAQ separately gives 190°C; our pilot explicitly uses the method, not a blend). Our quantities, spices and timing are adaptations requiring kitchen validation.

## Storage and transport

[FSA rice guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/home-food-fact-checker/home-food-fact-checker): cool promptly (ideally within an hour), use refrigerated rice within 24 hours of its original cooking time and reheat only once. For this meal-prep flow use fresh rice/filling, cool separately and assemble later. Previously reheated rice is for immediate eating; do not start a new storage clock. Chill at 4°C or below; promptly freeze portions intended for later. Thaw in the fridge. Reheat components until steaming throughout before assembly. [FSA chilled transport](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/food-safety-in-a-heatwave-advice-for-consumers/food-safety-in-a-heatwave-advice-for-consumers) supports insulated bags and ice packs. Foil maintains shape, not refrigeration.

Chicken cues follow [FSA cooking guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cooking-your-food/cooking-your-food). Cut into multiple thick pieces and check a steaming-hot centre, no pink and clear juices; cook longer if uncertain. No mandatory thermometer was added, in line with the user's existing preference.
